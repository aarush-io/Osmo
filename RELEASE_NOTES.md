Dial 1.8.0

### Podcasts
- There is a new Podcasts tab. It has a dial of shows, cards of recommended shows, and rows of episodes.
- A show has its own page. You can save a show, pin it to the dial, and filter its episodes: All, Unplayed, In progress or Played.
- New episodes have a "New episode" tag. The "Episodes for later" tile opens the episodes that you saved.
- You can select the source of the recommended shows: YT Music, Spotify or the two together.
- You can search for shows and episodes from the Podcasts tab.
- The player shows the video of an episode. It also has a button for the comments, the episode information, and Autoplay for the subsequent episode.
- Home and the main dial show only music.

### Home
- Category chips at the top of Home filter Home to one category.
- The artist dial shows on a new installation. It fills from your pins, your most played artists, your library and the Home feed.
- The "New music videos" row is removed, because it showed the same videos as "Music videos".
- The Feeling lucky die turns at a constant speed until the song starts. The subsequent song is prepared before you tap.
- Pages that open from a tile close back to the tile when Home moves.

### Search
- The Spotify, Library and Addons searches have the top result card and the filter tabs of the YouTube search.
- Results come in from top to bottom. Rows move smoothly when the list changes.
- The source bar and the selectors in Settings have one thumb that slides. You can drag it.

### Player
- A new "Hide status bar" switch in Settings, Player hides the status bar in the full player.
- The border of the mini player cover fades in while the player becomes small.
- The Canvas video search finds the video of the artist more accurately. It compares the length and the channel name.
- Pictures of videos load in a larger size where the app shows them large.
- A crash is corrected that could occur when you opened the Appearance settings or the full player.

### Settings
- Performance: you can set the video quality for Wi-Fi and for mobile data. The options are 480p, 1080p and Max quality.
- Performance: a new "Performance mode" switch decreases effects for slow phones. It also stops haptics.
- Performance: new switches are "Keep Home loaded", "Disable haptics" and "Static background colours".
- Performance: "High performance mode" does not change with the app style. If the battery setting prevents it, the row tells you, and a tap opens the setting.
- "Language & location" has a new Country setting. It sets the country for recommendations, search and radio.
- The default blur of the Glass style is 10dp. If you set a value before, that value stays.
- The sort menu of the Library has one row for each sort. A second tap changes the direction.
- The back button in the top bar has its full glass rim.

### Performance
- The app starts with less load. Explore and Library load after Home is ready.
- Home scrolls more smoothly. Rows are made before they come on the screen.
- Home stays loaded when you open a different page, thus it comes back immediately.
- The app does not request a refresh rate that the display refuses.
- "Enable logging" stays on after you close the app. The log has frame times, which helps to find the cause of stutter.

### Downloads
- Each release now has four APK files: arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a, x86_64 and universal.
- arm64-v8a is for most phones. armeabi-v7a is for phones with a 32-bit system.
- The update check in the app downloads the correct file for your phone.
